How do I get back a book I bought?
Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. Books shared with Family Sharing appear automatically.
How does my child become the star?
Tap Be the Star on the bookshelf, type a first name and, if you like, add a face photo. The photo never leaves your phone.
How do I remove a name, photo or clip?
Clear them in Be the Star, or delete clips in the Star moments gallery. Deleting the app removes everything.
Why does the story draw itself?
On iPhone Duo the page draws as you open the phone. Open it slowly to watch. Closed, you can read on the front screen.
Can I change the language or voice?
Yes. Settings has nine languages, the character voices, and the option to record your own.
A book was bought by mistake.
Apple handles all payments, so request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
